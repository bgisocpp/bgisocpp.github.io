  P2900 Contracts for C++ introduced the contracts facility in C++. Briefly,
  contracts allow us to state, in code, invariants that must hold during the
  execution of a program. Such invariants might be getting a sorted array as an
  input to std::binary_search or always receiving a non-negative number as a
  result of std::abs.


  Although P2900 was formally accepted and made part of C++ 26, it is still a controversial topic. Some committee members started a campaign to remove P2900 from the standard draft before releasing C++ 26 officially. Their arguments are stated in a series of papers [P4238R0](https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg21/docs/papers/2026/p4238r0.pdf), [P4332R0](https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg21/docs/papers/2026/p4332r0.html), [P4334R0](https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg21/docs/papers/2026/p4334r0.pdf).


  Our goal for this meeting is to introduce the contracts facility and the arguments for/against it to the wider public and have a debate on the topic. Long-time committee members Vassil Vassilev and Peter Dimov will join the discussion.
