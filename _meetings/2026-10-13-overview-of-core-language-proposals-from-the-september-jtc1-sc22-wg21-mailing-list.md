agenda: >-
  This meeting is an opportunity to review the 14 papers targeting CWG and EWG
  that were included in the September 2026 mailing list of JTC1/SC22/WG21. We
  will focus on the key ideas and directions rather than discussing the papers
  in detail. None of the papers has yet been formally accepted into the C++29
  draft, making this an ideal time to discuss the proposals and provide
  feedback.
