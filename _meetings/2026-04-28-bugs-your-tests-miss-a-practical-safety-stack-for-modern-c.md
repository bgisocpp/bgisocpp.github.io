  to surface later when they’re hardest to diagnose. He’ll show how to
  proactively uncover these issues with a practical modern C++ safety stack:
  stronger warnings, dual compiler lanes, static analysis, and sanitizers, along
  with the real-world trade-offs and gains at each step from simple compiler
  diagnostics to full runtime instrumentation."
